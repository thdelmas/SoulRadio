Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-08
Developer: Théophile Delmas-Leguery ("we")
Contact: contact@theophile.world

The short version

SoulRadio collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing about you off your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The app has no internet permission — it cannot transmit data even in principle.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage data, or listening history. We have no servers that receive data from the app.

Permissions and how data stays on your device

  • Location (optional): If you enable the solar schedule, SoulRadio reads your device location on the device only to calculate local sunrise and sunset times, so the 24-hour loop can follow the sun. This location is used in the moment and is never stored, logged, or transmitted. The app works fully without it (it falls back to clock time). You can deny or revoke this permission at any time.
  • No INTERNET permission: The app declares no network access. Nothing you do in SoulRadio leaves your device.

Audio you import

If you add your own audio files, they and the analysis SoulRadio derives from them stay entirely on your device. We never upload, copy, or transmit them.

Children

SoulRadio does not collect data from anyone, including children, and is suitable for all ages.

Third parties

There are no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics providers, or payment processors embedded in the app. Purchase of the app, where applicable, is handled by the app store (e.g. Google Play); refer to that store's own privacy policy for how it processes your purchase. SoulRadio itself never receives purchase or identity data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with a new effective date in the app's repository and store listing.
