## Full description

**A station for the body.**

SoulRadio is a 24-hour ambient radio you never have to tune. It knows it is 7 a.m. and
plays you 396 Hz. It knows it is 3 p.m. and lifts you to 528. It knows it is 11 p.m.
and lets you down gently into 174, and then into the Earth's own 7.83 Hz, and holds you
there until morning.

The morning wants clarity. The afternoon wants connection. The night wants to be let go
of. A single playlist applied to all of it is noise dressed as music. SoulRadio gives
the day its shape back — as wallpaper, not wallpaper-paste. Leave it on, forget it's
there, and still feel different at the end of the week.

**What's inside**
- A 24-hour auto-loop that follows the clock — or the sun, if you let it.
- A dial: nine frequencies plus companion tones, one tap each. No menus, no onboarding,
  nothing hidden behind an upsell.
- A Radio behind a deliberate door: a longer catalogue of chant, brainwave bands,
  planetary tones, noise colors and reconstructed ancient pieces — each labeled honestly.
- Your own library: bring your own audio and SoulRadio files it under a band on the dial.
- A curated library of 60+ recordings — Gregorian chant, Bach, Verdi-tuned strings,
  shakuhachi, raga, and pure tones — all under public-domain or Creative Commons licenses,
  credited in-app.

**What SoulRadio will never do**
- No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No accounts.
- It does not sell your listening data — because it has no internet permission and can't.
- No streaks, no notifications, no engagement traps. You are the listener, not the product.
- It will never pretend a frequency is a prescription. 528 Hz will not cure your back pain,
  and we will never claim it does. What sound reliably does — give the nervous system
  somewhere to rest, focus, or release — is enough.

You pay once for the app, and that is the whole transaction. SoulRadio is bought, not
extracted.

*Tune in. Or better — don't. Just leave it on.*
